AMOLED black wallpaper for foldables: dark pairs for the cover and inner screen

Looking for a pure black wallpaper? If you only need an all-black or solid color image, any tool that makes a solid color picture will do: save it to Photos and set it as your wallpaper in seconds. If you use a foldable and want both the cover screen and the inner screen in deep black, with a little more than a flat color, Foldable Wallpaper Studio has pure black glow pairs: one scene, a close-up on the cover screen and the whole view on the inner screen, each saved at its exact pixel size.

Two needs, two answers

  • Just pure black or a solid color: a single black image is enough. You do not need an app.
  • Dark on both screens of a foldable, with some depth: pick a pure black glow pair, so closed and open match and neither screen gets cropped or stretched.

Why black works so well on OLED

Each pixel on an OLED screen makes its own light, so black areas stay almost unlit. Blacks look deeper, contrast is higher, and icons and the clock stand out more.

Questions before you download

What is the fastest way to get a pure black wallpaper?
A pure black wallpaper is just an all-black image. Save any black image to Photos and set it as your wallpaper in Settings. If you want a little depth and detail on a dark background, look at the pure black glow pairs below.

Why do people use black wallpapers on OLED screens?
On an OLED screen each pixel makes its own light, so black areas stay almost unlit. Blacks look deeper, contrast is higher, and icons and the clock stand out. How much battery that saves depends on the device and how you use it, so this page gives no number.

Does a foldable need two different wallpapers?
Ideally, yes. The cover screen is tall and narrow, the inner screen is nearly square. The app exports 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen, both cut from the same artwork, so closed and open look like one design.

What is the pure black glow style?
It is one of the app's 14 styles, built on a pure black background with soft glowing light. Every set comes as a pair: one image for the cover screen and one for the inner screen.

Can I recolor a set or use my own photo?
Yes. Recolor with swatches and add one short line of text, with the closed and open previews redrawn instantly. You can also turn your own photo into a pair and drag to set the focus. The system photo picker is used, so the app never reads your whole library.

How do I set it as my wallpaper?
Both images are saved to Photos with add-only access. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so after you save, the Settings tab shows how to set each screen in a few steps.
