Foldable Wallpaper Studio: materials for editors

The story

A foldable has two screens, but most wallpapers are made for one. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes them in pairs for the two screens of iPhone Duo: close the phone and the cover screen shows a close-up; open it and the inner screen shows the panorama. Both images are cut from the same original artwork, so the two screens read as one picture. The library holds 300+ original pairs in 14 styles, and five widgets are drawn with the wallpaper you pick, in matching colors. Every pair can be previewed closed and open on any iPhone or iPad before it is saved.

Design points

  • Composed for both screens. The cover screen gets a close-up at 1398 x 2034 and the inner screen gets the full scene at 2853 x 2007, saved to Photos as two images.
  • One artwork, two cuts. Every set is a true pair: the close-up and the panorama come from the same original artwork.
  • Closed and open previews. Both views are shown in the app before anything is saved. Color swatches and one line of your own text redraw both previews instantly.
  • Your own photo as a pair. In the Create tab, pick a photo and drag to set the focus. The system photo picker is used, so the app never reads the whole library.
  • Five widgets from the wallpaper. My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card, in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.
  • Original artwork, private by design. All artwork is original, with no characters or works from other brands. No ads, no account and no data collected.

The in-app purchase

Without a purchase you can try every tool: HD export, recoloring, text, photo pairs and the basic widget styles, with 5 wallpaper sets ready to use. Images saved from those sets and from your own photos carry a small watermark. All five widgets can be added and work without a purchase. Duo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that opens all the remaining launch sets, removes the watermark and adds extra widget styles.
