Why one image cannot fit both screens

  • Book-style folds: the two screens have different shapes; on many models the cover screen is tall and narrow and the inner screen is much wider. The Galaxy Z Fold7, for example, goes from 2520 x 1080 outside to 2184 x 1968 inside. A single picture gets cropped two different ways.
  • Flip phones: the cover screen is small and almost square (1048 x 948 on the Galaxy Z Flip7), while the main screen is a long portrait. A portrait wallpaper loses most of its height on the cover.
  • Same generation, different sizes: the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra do not share a cover screen size, so a wallpaper sized for one is not sized for the other.

Picking the right image size

  1. Find your model in the table and note both resolutions.
  2. For each screen, use an image with the same proportions and at least as many pixels. Larger is fine; smaller will be enlarged and can look soft.
  3. Keep the subject away from the edges. Your phone decides the final visible area and may shift the image for the clock or for wallpaper effects.
  4. Preview both screens after setting, closed and open.

Setting a different wallpaper on each screen

For the Galaxy Z Fold8, Samsung's support page says: "Choose different wallpapers for the cover screen and the main screen". Its steps: touch and hold an empty area of the Home screen, tap Wallpaper and style, then select a wallpaper for the cover screen, main screen or Lock screen (Samsung support, Galaxy Z Fold8). For the Galaxy Z Flip8, Samsung's support page says to touch and hold the Cover screen to edit your wallpaper (Samsung support, Galaxy Z Flip8). Menus on older models and on other brands can differ, so check the maker's help pages for your model; this page does not list steps we could not find in an official source.

Questions

What size should a Galaxy Z Fold7 wallpaper be?
Samsung lists the Galaxy Z Fold7 main screen at 2184 x 1968 pixels and the cover screen at 2520 x 1080 pixels. The two screens have very different shapes, so use a separate image for each one, at least as large as the screen it is for.

What size is the iPhone Duo wallpaper?
Apple lists the iPhone Duo inner display at 1878 x 2670 pixels and the outer display at 1398 x 2034 pixels. An image with the same proportions and at least that many pixels will not need to be stretched.

Can one wallpaper fit both screens of a foldable phone?
Not without cropping. On a book-style foldable the cover screen and the inner screen have different shapes, so the phone has to cut a single image differently for each one. Two images made for the two shapes avoid that.

Can I set a different wallpaper on the cover screen and the main screen?
On the Galaxy Z Fold8, yes: Samsung's support page says you can choose different wallpapers for the cover screen and the main screen under Wallpaper and style. For other models and brands, check the maker's own help pages for your model.
