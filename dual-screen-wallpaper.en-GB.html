Dual screen wallpaper (UK): one scene, made to fit the cover screen and the inner screen

A foldable has a tall, narrow cover screen and a wide inner screen. One ordinary wallpaper on both usually crops the subject away outside and stretches it inside. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes every dual screen wallpaper as a pair cut from the same scene: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole panorama for the inner screen.

View on the App Store View on Google Play

What you get in the app

  • Pairs in many styles, from illustrated scenery and auroras to calm minimal textures and pure black glow for OLED.
  • Closed and open previews before you save.
  • Colour swatches and one short line of text, with both previews updating as you go.
  • A Create tab that turns your own photo into a pair: drag to set the focal point and a favourite holiday photo keeps its subject where you want it.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on iPhone that take their colours from the wallpaper you chose.

Setting a different image on each screen

On Android (Google Play):

  1. Save both images of a pair to your gallery.
  2. Open the cover image, tap Set as Wallpaper and choose Home screen, Lock screen or both for the cover screen.
  3. Do the same with the inner image for the inner screen. On a phone with one screen, either image works as a regular wallpaper.

On iPhone (App Store): the app saves the pair to Photos as two images, at 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen, the exact sizes of the two screens on Apple's foldable iPhone; on other models iOS scales them. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper for you, so a short guide in the app's Settings tab walks you through setting the cover and inner screens yourself.

Honest limits and purchases

  • No app can set your wallpaper automatically; the last taps are in your phone's settings.
  • Without a purchase, 5 pairs are ready to save and you can make pairs from your own photos; those saves carry a small watermark.
  • The full library of 300+ pairs comes with a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, which unlocks every other pair and removes the watermark.

Questions before you download

Can each screen have its own wallpaper?
Yes. Every pair is saved as two separate images, so the close-up goes on the cover screen and the full scene on the inner screen.

Is this an Apple or Google app?
No. Foldable Wallpaper Studio is made by an independent developer, Lumi Studio.

Do I need an account, or will I see ads?
No. There are no ads, no account, no sign-in and no tracking, and your photos stay on your phone.
