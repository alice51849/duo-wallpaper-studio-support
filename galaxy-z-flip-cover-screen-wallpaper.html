Galaxy Z Flip cover screen wallpaper that matches the inside

On a flip phone the cover screen is what you see all day: the time, a notification, a quick glance. It is small, so a regular wallpaper either loses its subject or looks unrelated to the one on the main screen. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes every set as a pair from the same original artwork: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen. Close the phone and you see the detail; open it and the whole picture is there.

What people want from a cover screen wallpaper

  • A subject that still reads on a small screen, with the clock on top
  • The same scene continuing when the phone opens, not two random images
  • Calm looks that sit well behind the time: pure black Amoled glow, calm mono, skyline glow, rainy night windows
  • Your own photo split into a cover and inner pair

How to set it up on Android (Google Play)

  1. Pick a set from 300+ in 14 styles, or turn your own photo into a pair and drag to set the focus.
  2. Check the closed and open previews, plus the lock screen clock preview, so the time does not cover the subject.
  3. Optional: change colors and add one line of text, with 5 fonts and control over size, opacity and position.
  4. Save both images to your gallery, tap Set as Wallpaper and choose Home screen, Lock screen or both.
  5. Put the cover image on the cover screen and the inner image on the inner screen. On some phones the cover screen wallpaper is picked from the phone's own cover screen settings.

Favorites and recently viewed sets make it easy to come back to a pair you liked.

On iPhone

The App Store version is made for the cover and inner screens of Apple's foldable iPhone. It saves both images to Photos at 1398 x 2034 (cover) and 2853 x 2007 (inner), shows closed and open previews on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and adds five widgets drawn from your wallpaper. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper, so the app shows you how to set each image.

Honest limits

  • No app can set your wallpaper for you automatically; you choose the image and the screen.
  • Cover screens differ in size and shape between models, and your phone decides exactly what part of the image shows. Sets with a centered subject crop best; check the preview first.
  • The cover image is a deliberate close-up, not a shrunken copy of the inner image.
  • Without a purchase you can save 5 sets and pairs made from your own photos, with a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, unlocks every set and removes the watermark.

Questions before you download

Can the cover screen have a different wallpaper from the main screen?
Yes. Each pair is saved as two separate images, so the close-up goes on the cover screen and the full scene on the inner screen, both cut from the same artwork.

Will the wallpaper get cut off on the small cover screen?
The cover image is already cropped as a close-up around the subject, but each phone decides the final visible area. Use the closed preview and the lock screen clock preview, and pick a set with a centered subject.

Does the app need an account or special permissions?
No. There is no account, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking, and the Android version asks for no special permissions. Your photos stay on your device.
