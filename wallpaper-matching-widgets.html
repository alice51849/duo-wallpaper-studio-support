Widgets drawn with your wallpaper

A Home Screen looks put together when the widgets and the wallpaper share one palette. Most widget apps make you match colors by hand. In the App Store version of Foldable Wallpaper Studio, the five widgets are drawn with the wallpaper you choose, so the clock, calendar and text card already belong to the same design as the background.

The five widgets

  • My Wallpaper: your chosen wallpaper as a widget.
  • Art Clock: the time, drawn with the wallpaper.
  • Calendar: the date in the same colors.
  • Battery: shows only this iPhone or iPad's charge level and charging state.
  • Text Card: a short line of your own text.

All five come in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes and can be added and used without a purchase. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, adds extra widget styles, such as more clock and battery styles, along with every remaining wallpaper set.

Set it up

  1. Pick a wallpaper set from 300+ originals in 14 styles, or make one from your own photo.
  2. Recolor it or add one line of text; the previews redraw instantly.
  3. Save the wallpaper to Photos and set it (iOS does not let apps set wallpapers, so the Settings tab shows the steps).
  4. Add the widgets from the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget gallery.

Every set is made in pairs for foldables: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen. It also works on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. On Android foldables, the Google Play version makes the same matching wallpaper pairs.

Questions before you download

Do the widgets change when I change my wallpaper?
The widgets are drawn with the wallpaper you choose in the app, so picking a different set gives them that set's look.

Do I need a foldable iPhone to use the widgets?
No. The app runs on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and the widgets come in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.

What does the Battery widget show?
Only this iPhone or iPad's charge level and charging state.

Are the widgets available on Android?
The five widgets are part of the App Store version. On Android foldables, use the Google Play version for the matching cover and inner wallpaper pairs.
