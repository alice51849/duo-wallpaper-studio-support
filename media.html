Foldable Wallpaper Studio
Press & media

Wallpapers made in pairs for the two screens of a foldable phone: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork.

為摺疊手機兩個螢幕成對製作的桌布：合上時外螢幕是特寫，展開時內螢幕是整片全景，兩張出自同一幅原創畫面。

Key features

  • 300+ original wallpaper sets in 14 styles, each a true pair for the cover screen and the inner screen
  • Closed and open previews before you save
  • Recolor a set and add one short line of your own text
  • Turn your own photo into a pair and drag to set the focus
  • All artwork is original and comes with the app
  • No ads, no account, no tracking; your photos stay on your device
  • Free to download with 5 sets ready to use; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks every set and removes the watermark
