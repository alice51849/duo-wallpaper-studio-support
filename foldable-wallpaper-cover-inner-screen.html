Foldable wallpaper, made in pairs

Looking for wallpaper that fits a foldable phone? Most wallpapers fit one screen, but a foldable has two. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes them in pairs: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork. Close it and the close-up shows on the cover; open it and the full scene continues on the inner screen.

What you get

  • 300+ original sets in 14 styles, including illustrated scenery, starry skies, auroras, cute clay and 3D, macaron pastels and pure black glow for OLED
  • Closed and open previews before you save
  • Recolor a set and add one short line of text
  • Pairs from your own photos, with the focus where you want it
  • Pixel-exact export: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen; on other models, iOS scales the image
  • Five widgets drawn with your wallpaper: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card
  • No ads, no account, no sign-in, no tracking

For iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Using an Android foldable? There is a Google Play version too.

Questions before you download

What is a foldable wallpaper?
A foldable phone has two screens with very different shapes, so one image rarely fits both. A foldable wallpaper is made as a pair from the same artwork: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen.

Why does my wallpaper look cut off or stretched on a foldable?
The cover and inner screens have different proportions. At the sizes this app exports, the cover image is 1398 x 2034 pixels and the inner image is 2853 x 2007 pixels, so a single picture ends up cropped on one screen or stretched on the other.

Can I check both screens before saving?
Yes. Closed and open previews show the cover screen opening into the inner screen before you save. Recoloring and one line of your own text redraw both previews instantly.

Can I turn my own photo into a pair?
Yes. In the Create tab, pick a photo with the system photo picker and drag to set the focus. The app never reads your whole library.

How do I set the wallpapers?
The two images are saved to Photos with add-only access. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so the Settings tab shows how to set each screen in a few steps.
