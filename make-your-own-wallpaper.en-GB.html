A holiday snap, your dog, a favourite street: lots of people would rather make their own wallpaper than pick one from a gallery. On a foldable there is a catch. The cover screen and the inner screen have very different proportions, so a single photo ends up cropped or stretched on one of them. Foldable Wallpaper Studio turns your own photo into a pair instead: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen.

How to make your own wallpaper

  1. Open the Create tab and pick a photo with the system photo picker, so the app never reads your whole library.
  2. Drag to set the focal point – a face, a pet, a landmark – so it stays in view on the narrow cover screen.
  3. Check the closed and open preview before you save.
  4. Save both images to Photos or your gallery, then set them in your phone's Settings: the cover image on the cover screen, the inner image on the inner screen.

What you get

  • Two separate images cut from the same photo, each framed for its own screen
  • On iPhone, exact pixel export in HD: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen; on other models iOS scales the images
  • An optional short line of text and colour swatches, with both previews updating instantly
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that take their colours from your wallpaper

Rather not make your own? There are also 300+ ready-made pairs in many styles – see foldable wallpaper pairs.

Honest limits

  • The app saves the images; you set them as your wallpaper yourself in your phone's Settings. On iPhone, iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically.
  • Without a purchase, images saved from your own photos and from the 5 ready-to-use pairs carry a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, removes the watermark and unlocks the other pairs.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.

Questions before you download

Can I use my own photo as the wallpaper for the cover and inner screen?
Yes. The app turns your photo into two images: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen. You drag to set the focal point.

Will my own wallpaper have a watermark?
Without a purchase, yes, a small one. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, removes it.

Does the app upload my photos?
No. You pick photos with the system photo picker, and the app has no servers, no account and no tracking.
