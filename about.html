Foldable Wallpaper Studio in four answers

Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes wallpapers in pairs for the two screens of a foldable phone: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork. It is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for fold and flip phones.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What is Foldable Wallpaper Studio?

  • A wallpaper app that makes every set as a true pair: close it and the cover screen shows a close-up, open it and the inner screen shows the whole scene.
  • 300+ original sets in 14 styles, from illustrated scenery and soft 3D to pure black for OLED and macaron colors. All artwork is original, with no characters or works from other brands.
  • Closed and open previews before you save, color swatches and one line of your own text, and a Create tab that turns your own photo into a pair with the focus where you want it.
  • App Store version: pixel-exact export for Apple's foldable iPhone (1398 x 2034 for the cover screen, 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen) and five widgets drawn with your wallpaper: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card.
  • No ads, no account and no tracking. Your photos stay on your device.

Who is it for?

  • People with Apple's foldable iPhone who want the cover screen and the inner screen to look like one design. Use the App Store version.
  • People with a fold or flip Android phone. Use the Google Play version, made for the cover and inner screens of foldable and flip phones.
  • People who do not have a foldable yet: the closed and open previews run inside the app on any iPhone or iPad, and on any other Android phone either image works as a regular wallpaper.
  • People who want a travel or family photo as a pair, or a Home Screen, Lock Screen and widgets in one color.

How is it paid for?

  • App Store: without a purchase you can try every tool (HD export, recoloring, text, photo pairs and the basic widget styles), with 5 wallpaper sets ready to use. Images saved from those sets and from your own photos carry a small watermark. All five widgets can be added and work without a purchase. Duo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that opens all the remaining launch sets, removes the watermark and adds extra widget styles.
  • Google Play: without a purchase you get 5 sets ready to save, and you can turn your own photos into pairs; these saves carry a small watermark. Studio Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, that unlocks every set and removes the watermark.

How is it different from other wallpaper apps?

  • Most wallpaper apps offer single wallpapers made for one screen. On a foldable, one image either loses its subject on the narrow cover screen or gets stretched on the wide inner screen.
  • Here every set is composed twice from the same artwork, one image per screen, so the closed and open views belong together.
  • You see both views before you save, and on iPhone the widgets are drawn with the same wallpaper.
  • Apps cannot change your wallpaper on their own on iOS or Android, so you choose which image goes on which screen; the iOS app shows the steps after you save.
