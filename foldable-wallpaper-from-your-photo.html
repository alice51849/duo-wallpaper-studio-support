Turn your own photo into a wallpaper for both foldable screens

A favorite photo rarely survives a foldable as-is. The tall, narrow cover screen crops the person or pet out of frame, and the near-square inner screen zooms the image until it looks soft. In the Create tab of Foldable Wallpaper Studio, one photo becomes a pair: a close-up framed for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen. You decide the focus by dragging.

How it works

  1. Open the Create tab and pick a photo. The app uses the system photo picker, so it never reads your whole library.
  2. Drag to set the focus so your subject sits in the cover-screen close-up.
  3. Check the closed and open previews. Add one line of text if you like (5 fonts, size, opacity, position).
  4. On Android (Google Play), save both images to your gallery and set each one on its screen. On iPhone and iPad, the pair is saved to Photos at 1398 x 2034 and 2853 x 2007; iOS does not let apps set wallpapers, so the Settings tab shows the steps.

Photos that work best

  • A clear subject with room around it: the subject goes on the cover screen, the setting fills the inner screen.
  • A subject near the middle: the cover screen also shows the clock and notifications.
  • Enough resolution: the inner screen is large, so small images can look blurry.

Honest limits

  • Your phone decides the final visible area and may shift the image slightly for the clock. Check the previews before you save.
  • Without a purchase, wallpapers made from your own photos carry a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, removes it and unlocks all 300+ original sets.

Your photos stay on your device: no ads, no account, no servers.

Questions before you download

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. The app has no servers and no account; photos are processed on your device, and picking one uses the system photo picker.

Can I choose what shows on the cover screen?
Yes. After you pick a photo, drag to set the focus. The cover close-up centers on it and the inner screen keeps the whole scene.

Can I add text to my photo wallpaper?
Yes, one line with 5 fonts and adjustable size, opacity and position. Both previews update right away.

Does it work on Android foldables?
Yes, use the Google Play version. Save both images to your gallery and set the cover image on the cover screen and the inner image on the inner screen.
