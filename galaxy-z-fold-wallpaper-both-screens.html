A Galaxy Z Fold wallpaper that fits both screens

A book-style foldable has a tall, narrow cover screen and an inner screen that is close to square. Set one image on both and something gives: the inner screen zooms a portrait image until only the middle is left, or the cover screen cuts a wide image down to a strip, and the two never seem to line up. The fix is not one image stretched twice but two images made for two screens. Foldable Wallpaper Studio cuts every set from the same original artwork: a close-up framed for the cover screen and the whole scene framed for the inner screen.

Why one wallpaper looks wrong on a Fold

  • Different shapes: a portrait crop for the cover screen does not fill a near-square inner screen without zooming.
  • Different framing: a subject in the center of the inner screen can end up at the edge of the cover screen, which reads as misaligned.
  • Different moments: you glance at the cover screen closed and use the inner screen open, so each one needs its own composition.

Set a matching pair on Android (Google Play)

  1. Choose a set from 300+ (600+ wallpapers) in 14 styles, from illustrated scenery and auroras to soft 3D clay and macaron colors, or make a pair from your own photo and drag to set the focus.
  2. Compare the closed and open previews side by side, and check the lock screen clock preview.
  3. Recolor or add one line of text if you like (5 fonts, size, opacity, position).
  4. Save both images to your gallery, tap Set as Wallpaper and pick Home screen, Lock screen or both.
  5. Assign the cover image to the cover screen and the inner image to the inner screen.

On Apple's foldable iPhone

The App Store version saves the pair to Photos at 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen, previews both on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and includes five matching widgets. iOS does not let apps set wallpapers, so the app's settings show the steps.

Honest limits

  • The two images are intentionally different framings of one artwork, not pixel-identical copies. If you want literally the same crop on both screens, this is not that.
  • Your phone decides the final visible area and may shift the image slightly for the clock or for wallpaper effects. Check the previews before you save.
  • The app cannot set wallpapers on its own; the last tap is always yours.
  • Without a purchase you can save 5 sets and your own photo pairs with a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, unlocks every set and removes the watermark.

Questions before you download

Why does my wallpaper look zoomed on the inner screen of my Fold?
The inner screen is close to square, so a portrait wallpaper has to be enlarged to fill it and the edges are cut. A pair gives the inner screen its own wide framing of the scene instead.

Will the cover and inner wallpapers line up?
They come from the same artwork, so color, style and subject match. The cover image is a close-up and the inner image shows the whole scene, which is how the pair stays readable on both shapes.

Can I use my own photo on both screens?
Yes. Pick a photo, drag to set the focus, and the app makes a cover and inner pair from it. Your photos stay on your device.
