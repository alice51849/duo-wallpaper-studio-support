Cover screen wallpaper on Android: one pair, two screens

Get it on Google Play View on the App Store

An Android fold or flip phone has a small cover screen on the outside and a large inner screen. Most let you give each screen its own wallpaper, but many wallpapers are made for a single screen, so they look cut off on one side or stretched on the other. Foldable Wallpaper Studio saves every set as two images from the same original artwork: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen.

Set it up in 4 steps

  1. Pick a set. Browse 300+ sets in 14 styles, or open Create to turn your own photo into a pair and drag to set the focus.
  2. Preview it. Check the closed and open previews and the lock screen clock preview. Change the colors or add one line of text (5 fonts, size, opacity and position) if you like.
  3. Save both images of the set to your gallery.
  4. Set each screen. In your gallery, open the cover image, tap Set as Wallpaper and choose Home screen, Lock screen or both, then put it on the cover screen. Do the same with the inner image for the inner screen. Menu names can differ a little between phones.
  • Cover image: a close-up, so the small outside screen shows one clear subject
  • Inner image: the full scene, so the open phone shows the whole picture
  • Same artwork: closing and opening the phone feels like one design

Styles that work well on a cover screen

Pure black Amoled glow, calm mono and macaron colors stay clean on a small screen with a clock on top. Illustrated scenery, natural wonders and auroras, twilight and cloud light, and skyline glow open up into a wide view on the inner screen. The 14 styles also include soft 3D clay and felt, rainy night windows, flowers, painterly art, fantasy night realms, vivid textures and warm lantern lanes. All artwork is original and comes with the app.

Honest limits

  • The app does not change your wallpaper by itself. You choose the image and the screen.
  • Without a purchase you get 5 sets ready to save and can turn your own photos into pairs; these saves carry a small watermark.
  • Steps in your phone's settings can look slightly different depending on the maker and system version.

Questions before you download

Can I use a different wallpaper on the cover screen and the inner screen on Android?
On most Android fold and flip phones, yes. The cover screen and the inner screen can each take their own image, which is why the app saves every set as two images: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen.

Does the app set the wallpaper for me?
No. Apps cannot change your wallpaper on their own, so you always choose the image and where it goes. The app saves both images to your gallery, and you set each one from there.

Will the two screens match?
Yes. Both images in a set are cut from the same original artwork, so you see a close-up when the phone is closed and the whole scene when it is open.

Can I make a cover screen wallpaper from my own photo?
Yes. In Create, pick a photo and drag to set the focus, and the app turns it into a pair. Without a purchase these saves carry a small watermark.

Do I need an account, and are there ads?
No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no special permissions. Your photos stay on your device.

Is it a subscription?
No. Without a purchase you get 5 sets ready to save, and you can turn your own photos into pairs. Studio Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks every set and removes the watermark.

I only have one screen. Does it still work?
Yes. On any other phone, either image of a set works as a regular wallpaper.
