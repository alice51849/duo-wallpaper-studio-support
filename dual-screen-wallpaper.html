Dual screen wallpaper: questions and answers

View on the App Store Get it on Google Play

A foldable phone has two screens with very different shapes, so one wallpaper rarely fits both. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes dual screen wallpapers in pairs: close it and you see a close-up on the cover screen, open it and you see the whole scene on the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork. Below are the questions people ask most before they download.

Quick answers

  • What you get: 300+ original pairs in 14 styles, plus pairs made from your own photos
  • Exact sizes on iPhone and iPad: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen, 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen
  • Preview first: closed and open previews before you save
  • Make it yours: color swatches and one short line of your own text
  • Matching widgets: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card, drawn with your wallpaper
  • Privacy: no ads, no account, no tracking; your photos stay on your device

How to set a dual screen wallpaper

  1. Pick a pair, or make one from your own photo and drag to set the focus.
  2. Check the closed and open previews, and recolor or add a line of text if you like.
  3. Save both images to Photos or your gallery.
  4. Set the cover image on the cover screen and the inner image on the inner screen. On iPhone, iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so the Settings tab walks you through it.

Questions before you download

What is a dual screen wallpaper?
It is a wallpaper made as two images, one for each screen of a foldable phone: a close-up for the narrow cover screen and the full scene for the wide inner screen, both cut from the same artwork so the two screens look like one design.

Why not use the same image on both screens?
The two screens have very different proportions. The cover screen is tall and narrow, while the inner screen is wider than it is tall, so one image ends up cropped on one screen or enlarged on the other.

What sizes does the app export?
On iPhone and iPad, the app saves two images to Photos: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen. On other models, iOS scales the images.

Can I make a dual screen wallpaper from my own photo?
Yes. In the Create tab, pick a photo with the system photo picker and drag to set the focus. Without a purchase, images saved from your own photos carry a small watermark.

Does the app set the wallpaper for me?
No. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so after you save, the Settings tab shows how to set each screen in a few steps.

Is it a subscription?
No. Without a purchase you can use every tool and 5 wallpaper sets. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the remaining sets and removes the watermark.
