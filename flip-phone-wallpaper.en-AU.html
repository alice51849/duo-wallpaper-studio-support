Flip phone wallpaper (Australia): a cover screen close-up that matches the inside

Get it on Google Play (Australia) Download on the App Store

A flip phone shows a small cover screen when it is closed and a full screen when you open it. A wallpaper made for one screen rarely suits both. Foldable Wallpaper Studio on Google Play makes each wallpaper as a pair for fold and flip phones: a close-up for the cover screen and the whole scene for the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork.

What you get for a flip phone

  • True pairs: 300+ sets, 600+ wallpapers in 14 styles, each with a cover image and an inner image
  • Preview first: closed and open previews before you save, plus a lock screen clock preview
  • Make it yours: change the colours and add one line of text, with 5 fonts, size, opacity and position
  • Your own photos: turn a photo into a pair and drag to set the focus
  • Handy extras: favourites and recently viewed sets
  • Privacy: no account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking and no special permissions; your photos stay on your device

How to set a flip phone wallpaper

  1. Pick a set, or create one from your own photo.
  2. Check the closed preview so the subject sits well on the cover screen, and the lock screen clock preview so the time does not cover it.
  3. Save both images of the set to your gallery.
  4. Tap Set as Wallpaper and choose Home screen, Lock screen or both. Put the cover image on the cover screen and the inner image on the inner screen.

Apps cannot change your wallpaper on their own, so you always choose the image and where it goes. On a phone that does not fold, either image works as a regular wallpaper.

Honest limits

  • Your phone decides the exact visible area of the cover screen; check the closed preview before saving.
  • Without a purchase you get 5 sets ready to save, and you can turn your own photos into pairs; these saves carry a small watermark. Studio Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, that unlocks every set and removes the watermark.

Questions before you download

Does it work on any flip phone?
It is made for the cover and inner screens of foldable and flip phones. Your phone decides the exact visible area, so check the closed preview first. On a phone that does not fold, either image works as a regular wallpaper.

Can the app set the wallpaper for me?
No. Apps cannot change your wallpaper on their own. You save both images to your gallery, tap Set as Wallpaper and choose where each one goes.

Is it a subscription?
No. Studio Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks every set and removes the watermark.
