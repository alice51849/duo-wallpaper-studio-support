Wallpaper for a Pixel Fold: getting a matching pair to work across home and lock screens

If you've unfolded your Pixel 9 Pro Fold or Pixel 10 Pro Fold and found the wallpaper you picked for the outside looks oddly cropped on the big screen (or the other way round), you're not doing anything wrong. The two displays are very different shapes, and one picture has to be cropped to fit both.

What your Pixel actually lets you choose

When you touch and hold the home screen and open Wallpaper & style, the choice you're given is where the image goes: Home screen, Lock screen, or both. We couldn't find anything in Google's own help pages about setting a separate image for the outer screen and another for the inner screen. In practice, the same image gets framed to suit whichever screen you're looking at.

That's different from some other foldables, where the settings let you set the cover screen on its own. So on a Pixel, the trick is to work with home and lock rather than outer and inner.

Heads up: these menus can change with software updates. If your Pixel ever offers an option for each screen, use it and put the cover image on the outer screen and the inner image on the inside.

Three ways to use a matching pair on a Pixel foldable

  1. Whole scene at home, close-up on the lock screen. Set the inner image (the full scene) as your home screen and the cover close-up as your lock screen. You'll see the close-up when you wake the phone and the full scene once you unlock, on whichever screen you're using.
  2. Flip it. If you mostly use the phone closed, try it the other way: the close-up on the home screen and the full scene on the lock screen.
  3. Use your own photo and pick the focus. Turn a photo into a pair and drag the focus point onto what matters, so the crop centres on a face or a landmark.

Before you save, the app shows a closed preview, an open preview and a lock screen clock preview, so you can check the clock won't sit on top of the one bit you care about.

Setting it, step by step

  1. Pick a set in the app and save both images to your gallery.
  2. Open the image you want on your home screen and choose Set as Wallpaper (or go to Wallpaper & style on your phone and pick it from your photos).
  3. Choose Home screen. Then do the same with the second image and choose Lock screen.
  4. Fold and unfold the phone a few times to see how each one sits.

The app doesn't set your wallpaper for you, so you always choose the image and where it goes.

What's in the app

  • Over 300 sets (more than 600 wallpapers), each made as a true pair, cut from the same original artwork
  • 14 styles, including pure black AMOLED glow, rainy night windows, auroras and natural wonders, soft 3D clay and felt, and warm lantern lanes
  • Change the colours and add one line of text, with 5 fonts and adjustable size, opacity and position
  • Favourites and recently viewed sets
  • There are no ads and you don't need an account. Nothing is tracked, the app asks for no special permissions, and your photos stay on your phone.

Without a purchase you get 5 sets ready to save and you can turn your own photos into pairs; those saves carry a small watermark. Studio Pro is a one-off in-app purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks every set without the watermark.

What it can't do

  • The app can't make your Pixel use two different images on the two displays. It gives you a well-matched pair, and you decide where each one goes.
  • The cover close-up is tall and narrow, so on the unfolded screen it will be cropped noticeably.
  • Exactly how the image is cropped depends on your model and Android version, so check it folded and unfolded.
  • The artwork is original to the app. It isn't Google's own wallpaper collection.
