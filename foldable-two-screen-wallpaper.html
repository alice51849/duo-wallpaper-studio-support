Wallpaper for both screens of a foldable phone

A foldable phone has two screens: a tall, narrow one on the outside that you see when it's closed, and a wide one on the inside that you see when it's open. One picture rarely fits both. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes wallpaper as a matched pair instead: a close-up for the outside screen and the full scene for the inside, both cut from the same artwork.

Preview it closed and open, on the device you have now

You don't need a foldable phone to start. The app runs on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later and shows closed and open previews of every pair. Browse, recolor and line up a pair on the phone or tablet in your hand, and check both halves before you save anything.

Choose a pair, or make one from your photo

From the library: 300+ original pairs across 14 styles, from night skies and pure black to clay-style 3D scenes, auroras and watercolor. Recolor any set with the color swatches or add one short line of your own text.

From your camera roll: in the Create tab, pick a photo and drag to set the focus. A shot with some room around the subject works best, since the inside screen shows the wide view. The app uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photo you choose.

Seasonal pairs for winter

The library includes golden autumn and snowy seasonal moments, so you can change both screens with the season. A winter wallpaper pair works like any other set: a close-up for the outside screen and the whole snowy scene for the inside screen, cut from the same artwork. Recolor the set with the color swatches or add one short line of your own text, and check the closed and open previews before you save. To keep the Home Screen in the same colors, add the Art Clock or one of the other widgets, which are drawn with the wallpaper you choose. For a winter example, see the Snow Globe pair in the wallpaper gallery.

How to set the pair on both screens

  1. Save the pair. Both images go into Photos as two separate files; the app only asks to add photos, not to read your library.
  2. Open the guide in the app's Settings tab. It walks you through setting each screen.
  3. Set the two images yourself. iOS doesn't let any app change your wallpaper automatically, so it takes a few steps.

Five widgets that match the wallpaper

To keep the rest of the screen from clashing, the app includes five widgets drawn with the wallpaper you choose:

  • My Wallpaper
  • Art Clock
  • Calendar
  • Battery: shows only this iPhone or iPad's own charge level and charging state
  • Text Card

They come in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.

What's free and what the purchase adds

Without paying, you can use every tool, including HD export, recoloring, text, photo pairs and the basic widget styles, with 5 sets ready to save; images saved from those sets and from your own photos carry a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, opens the remaining sets and removes the watermark. There are no ads, no account and no tracking.
