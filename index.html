Questions, bugs or ideas? Email hourstag.app@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version (Settings › General › About). We usually reply within two business days.

How do I set a wallpaper?

Open a set, tap Save, and the image goes to your Photos library. Then open Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper › Photos and pick it. On a foldable iPhone, save both screens of the pair and set each one on its own screen.

The app asks to add photos. Why?

Saving needs the "Add Photos Only" permission. The app cannot see or read your existing photos with it. When you build a wallpaper from your own photo, you choose it with the system photo picker, and only that one photo is shared with the app.

I bought Duo Pro on another device. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Duo Pro is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to renew.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A widget looks out of date.

iOS decides when widgets refresh. Opening the app, or long-pressing the widget and choosing Edit Widget, refreshes it.

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-20. Applies to the Foldable Wallpaper Studio app for iPhone and iPad.

Short version: we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your data to any server.

Photos

When you make a wallpaper from your own photo, you pick it with Apple's system photo picker; only the photo you choose is given to the app. It is processed on your device and never uploaded. To save wallpapers the app asks for "Add Photos Only" access, which lets it add new images but not read your library.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, recently viewed sets, your settings and your widget choices are stored only on your device (in the app's own storage and in a shared container that only this app and its widgets can read). Deleting the app deletes them.

Ratings

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Foldable Wallpaper Studio

Matched wallpaper pairs for the cover and inner screens of a foldable iPhone, plus wallpapers for every other iPhone and iPad. Everything is drawn on your device.

  • A large library of original wallpaper sets, each with its own palette; a few are free to use.
  • Turn your own photo into a matched pair for both screens.
  • Save straight to Photos, then set it in Settings › Wallpaper.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that follow your current wallpaper.
  • Duo Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the full library. No subscription, no ads.
