iPhone Duo wallpaper: one picture, two screens

"iPhone Duo" is one of the names people type when they're looking for wallpaper for Apple's foldable iPhone, so that's the name this page uses. What most of those searches are really after is the same thing: a wallpaper for the outside screen when the phone is closed, and one for the big inside screen when it's open, that actually go together. Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes exactly that, as a matched pair of images.

How the outside and inside images pair up

The outside (cover) screen is tall and narrow. The inside screen is wider than it is tall. Put one photo on both and it gets cropped two different ways, so the subject often ends up cut off on one of them. A pair works differently: both images come from the same artwork, and the outside one is a close-up of the scene you see in full when you open the phone. Close it, and you're looking at a detail. Open it, and the rest of the picture is there.

You don't need a folding iPhone to try it

The app runs on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and it shows closed and open previews right in the app. So you can browse, recolor and line up a pair on the phone you have today and see how both halves look before you save anything. On other models, iOS scales the images to fit.

Pick a pair, or build one from your photo

From the library: 300+ original pairs across 14 styles, from night skies and pure black to clay-style 3D scenes, auroras and watercolor. Recolor any set with the color swatches or add one short line of your own text.

From your camera roll: in the Create tab, pick a photo and drag to set the focus. A shot with some room around the subject works best, since the inside screen shows the wide view. The app uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photo you choose.

Tips for photos: turn your own photo into a foldable wallpaper.

Save two images, then set them yourself

When you save, both images go into Photos as two separate files; the app only asks to add photos, not to read your library. Then comes the part that surprises people: iOS doesn't let any app change your wallpaper automatically. You set each image yourself in a few steps, and the Settings tab in the app walks you through it for each screen.

Five widgets that match the wallpaper

To keep the rest of the screen from clashing, the app includes five widgets drawn with the wallpaper you choose: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card, in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes. The Battery widget shows only this iPhone or iPad's own charge level and charging state.

No ads, no account

There are no ads, no account and no tracking. In-app purchase details are on the app’s store page.
