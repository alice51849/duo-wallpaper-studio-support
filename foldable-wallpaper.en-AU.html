Foldable wallpaper in Australia: pick one pair for both screens

Download on the App Store (Australia) Get it on Google Play

Searching the App Store in Australia for a foldable wallpaper usually turns up images made for one screen. On a foldable, the tall cover screen and the wide inner screen need different crops, so Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes every wallpaper as a pair: a close-up for the cover screen and the full panorama for the inner screen, cut from the same scene.

A quick checklist before you save a foldable wallpaper

  • Does the subject survive the cover screen? Check the closed preview first; the close-up is framed for the tall, narrow screen.
  • Does the scene carry on when you open it? The open preview shows the whole panorama on the wide inner screen.
  • Does the colour suit you? Recolour with swatches and both previews update instantly.
  • Want a word or two on it? Add one short line of text.
  • Using your own photo? The Create tab turns it into a pair; drag to set the focal point, so a favourite holiday snap keeps its subject where you want it.

Styles to choose from

300+ original pairs, including Japanese-style illustrated scenery with summer clouds, seaside towns and starry skies; warm lantern lanes; golden autumn and snowy seasonal moments; fantasy realms with floating islands; cute clay and 3D; rainy windows and city lights at night; natural wonders and auroras; oil painting, woodblock print and watercolour; flowers and blossoms; calm minimal textures and solid colours; pure black glow for OLED; satin, foil and metal textures; and macaron pastel colours.

Saving and setting it on iPhone

  1. Choose a pair, or make one from your own photo. You pick images with the system photo picker, so the app never reads your whole library.
  2. Save both images to Photos at 1398 x 2034 (cover screen) and 2853 x 2007 (inner screen). The app only asks for add-only access. On other models, iOS scales the images.
  3. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so follow the short guide in the Settings tab to set the cover and inner screens.
  4. Optional: add the matching widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen (My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card), all drawn with the wallpaper you chose.

The closed and open preview works on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, so you can try a pair before you set it.

Honest limits

  • No app can set your wallpaper for you; the last step takes a few taps in Settings.
  • Without a purchase, 5 pairs are ready to use, and images saved from those pairs and from your own photos carry a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the other pairs, removes the watermark and adds extra widget styles.

Questions before you download

Is this app made by a phone maker?
No. Foldable Wallpaper Studio is made by an independent developer, Lumi Studio.

Can the cover screen have a different wallpaper from the inner screen?
Yes. Each pair is saved as two separate images: the close-up for the cover screen and the full panorama for the inner screen.

Do I need an account, or will I see ads?
No. There are no ads, no account, no sign-in and no tracking. The only network connection is for in-app purchases.
