“iPhone Duo” wallpaper? Foldable iPhone wallpaper (UK): a matching pair, not one stretched image

If you have been searching for a foldable wallpaper or an iPhone Duo wallpaper, the problem is usually the same: a single image made for one screen either crops away its subject on the narrow cover screen or looks unrelated once the phone opens. Foldable Wallpaper Studio turns one original image into a pair: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen.

How it works on iPhone (App Store)

  1. The App Store version is made for the cover and inner screens of Apple's foldable iPhone, and shows closed and open previews on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later.
  2. Pick a set from 300+ in 14 styles, or make a pair from your own photo; change colours or add one short line of text.
  3. Save both images to Photos at 1398 x 2034 (cover) and 2853 x 2007 (inner). The app only asks for permission to add photos.
  4. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper for you, so the guide in the app's settings shows how to set each image.

On Android foldables and flip phones (Google Play)

Save both images to your gallery, tap Set as Wallpaper and put the cover image on the cover screen and the inner image on the inner screen. Model-specific guides: Galaxy Z Flip cover screen · Galaxy Z Fold both screens.

Honest limits

  • No app can set your wallpaper automatically; the last step is always in your phone's settings.
  • Your phone decides the exact visible area; check the closed preview first.
  • Without a purchase you can save 5 sets and pairs made from your own photos, with a small watermark. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, unlocks every set and removes the watermark.

Questions before you download

Is this an Apple app?
No. Foldable Wallpaper Studio is made by an independent developer, Lumi Studio. The App Store version is designed for the cover and inner screens of Apple's foldable iPhone.

Can the cover screen have a different wallpaper from the inner screen?
Yes. Each pair is saved as two separate images, so the close-up goes on the cover screen and the full scene on the inner screen.

Do I need an account, or will I see ads?
No. There is no account, sign-in, ads or tracking. Your photos stay on your phone.
